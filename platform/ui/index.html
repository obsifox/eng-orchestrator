Location

The position reported to a page that asks for geolocation. A radius describes an area rather than a point, and the engine samples inside it.

Sampled positions

Ten consecutive resolutions of the active profile. A stable or seeded profile produces the same coordinate each time. A dynamic profile does not.

Timezone

The clock the browser reports. This is a browser-scoped setting. It does not change the operating system clock, and it does not change what other applications see.

Locale and language

Five surfaces are often collapsed into the word locale. They are kept apart here because a page can observe more than one of them, and they do not have to agree.
