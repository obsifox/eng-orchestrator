# Bad Plan Example (Anti-Pattern)

## Project: WooCommerce Bulk Discount

## Plan (BAD - do not do this)
- Build everything at once
- Use microservices for WordPress plugin
- 20 specialists: Blockchain Engineer, Quantum Engineer, etc
- No task graph, no dependencies
- No evidence refs
- No risks
- "Make it highly scalable and secure" (no measurable criteria)
- Will create 50 files for simple feature

## Why BAD
- No tier justification
- Role explosion (20 specialists for Tier 2 task)
- No file-disjoint proof, parallel conflict risk
- No acceptance criteria, no evidence plan
- Over-engineering: microservices for WP plugin
- No scope control, no REQUIRED vs BACKLOG
- No budget, no loop caps
- Persona theater: "As a Senior Blockchain Guru..."
- No baseline, no rollback plan
